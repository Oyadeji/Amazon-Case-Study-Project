###  Insights & Recommendations
Based on:

Average Discount %, Average Ratings, Price & Revenue Trends

Potential Revenue by Category, Price Range Distribution

## 1️⃣ Discount Patterns
Most categories (Car&Motorbike, Home&Kitchen, Toys&Games, etc.) have an average discount of ~70%.

Computers & Accessories has the lowest average discount (~69%), while Electronics and Health & Personal Care are slightly lower than 70%.

Interpretation:
Palmora seems to apply a uniform heavy-discount strategy. This likely drives sales volume but could impact profitability if costs are high.

## 2️⃣ Customer Satisfaction (Ratings)
Highest-rated categories: Office Products (~4.43), Toys & Games (~4.43), Home Improvement (~4.25) — all above 4.2.

Lowest-rated: Car&Motorbike (~3.8), Musical Instruments (~3.9).

Mid-range performers: Electronics (~4.1), Computers & Accessories (~4.15), Health & Personal Care (~4.0).

### Interpretation:
High-rated categories may benefit from strong quality control and customer experience.
Lower-rated ones might have quality issues, unclear product descriptions, or mismatched expectations.

## 3️⃣ Price Positioning
Highest Average Prices: Health & Personal Care (₹10K actual), Car&Motorbike (₹4K), Home&Kitchen (~₹4K).

Lowest Average Prices: Toys & Games, Office Products.

Significant gap between actual and discounted prices exists across all categories.

Interpretation:
High-value categories (like Health & Personal Care) can generate large individual sale revenue but may require strong trust/brand credibility.

## 4️⃣ Potential Revenue Leaders
Top 3: Electronics (490), Home&Kitchen (448), Computers & Accessories (375).

Minimal contribution: Car & Motorbike, Health & Personal Care, Toys & Games (all near 1), Home Improvement & Musical Instruments (2), Office Products (31).

Interpretation:
The largest revenue opportunities lie in Electronics, Home&Kitchen, and Computers & Accessories — either due to high sales volumes, high prices, or both.

## 5️⃣ Price Range Distribution
High-revenue categories also dominate higher price ranges, showing a direct link between price and potential revenue.

Low-performing categories rarely appear in premium price buckets.

Interpretation:
Pricing strategy is a major driver of revenue — premium-positioned products in demand categories yield the most impact.

📌 Strategic Recommendations
Double Down on Revenue Leaders

Prioritize Electronics, Home&Kitchen, and Computers & Accessories for marketing, inventory stocking, and promotional focus.

Improve Quality in Low-Rated Categories

Investigate negative feedback in Car&Motorbike and Musical Instruments; address quality, packaging, or service issues.

Reevaluate Discounting Strategy

Since discounts are uniformly high (~70%), test selective discounting for high-demand products to improve margins.

Expand High-Price Offerings in Mid-Performers

Introduce premium SKUs in categories like Office Products and Health & Personal Care to push them into higher price brackets.

Bundle Low-Performing Categories

Increase visibility of low-contribution categories by pairing them with top sellers (e.g., bundle Office Products with Computers & Accessories).

Monitor Price Sensitivity

Adjust prices based on sales performance — especially in Health & Personal Care and Home&Kitchen where prices are already high.
